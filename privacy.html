Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile application Extrasense (“the App”) handles user information.

The App provides entertainment content such as horoscopes, tarot readings, and dream interpretations.

Information Stored on the Device

The App allows users to optionally enter personal information such as a name and date of birth. This information is stored only locally on the user's device and is not transmitted to the developer's servers. It is used only to personalize certain features such as horoscope readings and compatibility results.

User Input

Some features of the App allow users to enter text, including:

  • Questions for tarot readings
  • Descriptions of dreams for interpretation

This information may be transmitted to third-party artificial intelligence services in order to generate a response requested by the user. The developer does not store this information.

Third-Party Services

The App uses third-party services to provide functionality and improve the user experience. These services may collect limited technical data automatically.

  • Firebase — used to retrieve horoscope content and support app functionality. Privacy Policy
  • DeepSeek — used to generate tarot readings and dream interpretations based on user input.
  • Appodeal — used to display advertisements and rewarded ads. Privacy Policy

Advertising partners may collect certain information automatically, including:

  • Advertising ID
  • IP address
  • Device information
  • Anonymous usage data

This information is used for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention purposes.

Advertising and Virtual Currency

The App displays advertisements. Users may receive virtual in-app currency called “Stars” as a reward for watching advertisements. This virtual currency is used only within the App to access certain features and has no real-world monetary value.

Entertainment Disclaimer

All horoscope content, tarot readings, and dream interpretations provided by the App are intended for entertainment purposes only and should not be considered professional advice.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect user information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
